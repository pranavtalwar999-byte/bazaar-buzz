Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Bazaar Buzz ("we") is a student-built platform that helps people discover offers from nearby shops and helps local businesses reach customers. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Email sign-up: if you create an account with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Content you post: offers, reels (videos), ratings and reviews, and the shop details you enter.
  • Activity: coupons you claim, reels you like and pack purchases (payments are processed by Razorpay; we never store card details).
  • Location: only if you tap "Use my GPS", and only inside your browser to sort deals by distance. We do not store your location.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show offers, reels and ratings to people nearby.
  • To let businesses see how their posts perform (counts only, never your personal details).

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your email with shops or other users.
  • We do not use your Google data for advertising.

Your choices

You can delete reels you posted at any time. To delete your account and data, email us and we will remove it within 7 days.
